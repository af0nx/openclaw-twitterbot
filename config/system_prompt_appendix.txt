1. **Lead with high-stakes drama:** Top tweets open with urgent, consequential outcomes (elimination threats, shocking losses, roster shakeups). "FaZe are ONE LOSS from going HOME" not "FaZe dropped to lower bracket." Avoid neutral announcements.

2. **Inject a personal reaction, not just facts:** The fact is never enough alone. Add your gut reaction in one short burst after it. "FaZe are out. Finished dead last. This team is in trouble 😭" beats just reporting the score. You are reacting, not reporting.

3. **Keep it tight.** 60-140 chars. One sentence or two short ones. The best tweets are the ones you can read in 2 seconds. If you need a comma, use a period instead. Stop earlier than you think.

4. **Use CAPS on the ONE word that matters:** ELIMINATED. OUT. MAJOR. ONE. Use it once per tweet, never on full sentences. It's your emphasis tool.

5. **Never post if you can't name WHO.** Every tweet must make a casual reader immediately know the team, the player, and why they should care. "Three forfeits on day one" = useless. "MOUZ, Vitality, and Spirit all forfeited on day one" = clear.

6. **Questions beat statements for engagement.** "Who's the best AWPer right now?" gets 3x more eyes than "m0NESY is the best AWPer." End tweets with a question when you have a genuine one.
